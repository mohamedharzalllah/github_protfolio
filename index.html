About

I'm a computer engineer finishing an M2 in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science at Université Paris-Dauphine (Tunis campus). My work centres on generative AI and agentic systems: LLM agents that route questions between document retrieval and structured data, natural-language interfaces over databases, and evaluation that shows whether a system actually works.

I currently work on IRIS Agent at IRIS IT, a multi-agent LLM document-intelligence platform for health-insurance brokerage. Alongside it, I've built projects in medical imaging, NLP, real-time data pipelines and combinatorial optimisation.

I'm open to AI / GenAI engineering roles (remote-friendly) and research positions in agentic AI.
